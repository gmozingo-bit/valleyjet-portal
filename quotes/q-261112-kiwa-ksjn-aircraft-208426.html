Hi Josh,

Most people don't know these aircraft by name until they've flown a few. Here's what actually separates them, so you can pick the one that feels right for a quick day trip.

All five make the roughly 135 nautical mile hop nonstop, under an hour in the air each way. Lavatory setups differ between these aircraft, so I've noted each one below.

The three things that really differ

Cabin heightWhether it's a walk-around cabin or a seated cabin you settle into, like first class.
Cabin widthHow much elbow and leg room there is between the facing seats.
Bag spaceHow much luggage fits, and whether you can reach your bags during the flight.

You're in the air about an hour, and you'll be seated for nearly all of it, just like a first-class seat. Height mostly matters for the walk to the lav.

My take

If you want the most room, it's the CJ4, as long as leaving from Scottsdale works for you.

If you'd like to leave from Mesa Gateway in a jet, the first Citation M2 is the one I'd look at.
