Most people don't know these aircraft by name until they've flown a few, so here's what actually separates them. With six of you and a few bags, cabin size and bag space are the two things worth comparing.

All five fly the roughly 220 nautical miles nonstop, about an hour in the air in the jets and a little over an hour in the PC-12. Lavatory setups differ between these aircraft, so I've noted each one below.

The three things that really differ

HeadroomWhether you can stand straight up or walk the aisle with a slight stoop.
Cabin widthHow much elbow and leg room there is between the facing seats.
Bag spaceHow much luggage fits, and whether you can reach your bags during the flight.

My take

If room for six and the bags matter most, it's the Citation XLS. It has the most cabin and the most bag space.

If you'd like the best value in a jet, the CJ4 is the one. It's the lowest-priced jet and comfortable for six.

If price is the main thing, the PC-12 comes in at a little over half the price of the lowest jet, landing at North Las Vegas.
