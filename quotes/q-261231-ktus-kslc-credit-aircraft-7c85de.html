Great question, and you're not alone. Most people don't know these jets by name until they've flown a few. Here's what actually separates them, so you can pick the one that feels right.

Every option flies the 523 nautical miles nonstop each way. The jets take about 1 hour 50 minutes to 2 hours, and the PC-12 about 2 and a half hours. Lavatory setups differ between these aircraft, so I've noted each one below.

The three things that really differ

Cabin heightWhether it's a walk-around cabin or a seated cabin you settle into, like first class.
Cabin widthHow much elbow and leg room there is between the facing seats.
Bag spaceHow much luggage fits, and whether you can reach your bags during the flight.

How the cabins feel

You're in the air about two hours, and you'll be seated for nearly all of it, just like a first-class seat. Height mostly matters for the walk to the lav.

About Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi varies from jet to jet, even within the same model, so I don't want to promise it on a type. Once you narrow it to one or two, I'll confirm whether that specific aircraft has it.

My take

If price matters most, it's the PC-12, and the CJ3 is the lowest-priced jet.

If you'd like wifi for the day, look at the Citation Ultra or the M2.

If you want room to get up and walk around, the Citation Excel is the roomiest cabin on the list.
