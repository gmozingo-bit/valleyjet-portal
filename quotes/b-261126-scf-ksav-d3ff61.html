Worth knowing before you choose. Scottsdale to Savannah is about 1,545 nautical miles, so a light jet will normally plan a quick fuel stop along the way, while a midsize or super-midsize makes the run nonstop in roughly three and a half hours. For a single traveler the light jet is the value play, and the midsize is what I'd pick if you'd rather not stop.
