Hi Jack,

Great question, and you're not alone. Most people don't know these jets by name until they've flown a few. Here's what actually separates them, so you can pick the one that feels right.

All of these fly the 908 nautical miles nonstop, about 3 hours in the jets and the Epic and just under 4 hours in the PC-12, with two pilots up front. Lavatory setups differ between these aircraft, so I've noted each one below.

The three things that really differ

Cabin heightWhether it's a walk-around cabin or a seated cabin you settle into, like first class.
Cabin widthHow much elbow and leg room there is between the facing seats.
Bag spaceHow much luggage fits, and whether you can reach your bags during the flight.

My take

If price matters most, it's the Epic E1000. It's the lowest number on the list and it's about as quick as the jets.

If you'd like a jet at the best price, it's the Citation M2 on Tuesday, November 10.
