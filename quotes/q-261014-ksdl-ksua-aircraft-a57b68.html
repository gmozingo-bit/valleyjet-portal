Hi John,

Great question, and you're not alone. Most people don't know these jets by name until they've flown a few. Here's what actually separates them, so you and your wife can pick the one that feels right.

All six fly the 1,680 nautical miles nonstop, about 3 hours 45 minutes to 4 hours 15 minutes in the air. All six have a private, enclosed lavatory, leather club seating, and two pilots up front. At this size there's no flight attendant, so drinks and snacks are stocked in the cabin for you.

The three things that really differ

HeadroomWhether you can stand straight up or walk the aisle with a slight stoop.
Cabin widthHow much elbow and leg room there is between the facing seats.
Bag spaceMatters for your checked bags and Pelican cases. Some jets let you reach your bags during the flight.

Super Midsize Jets

The bigger, faster end of your options.

Most room

Bombardier Challenger 300

$41,049  ·  Wed Oct 14  ·  seats up to 8 or 9

Stand-up cabinWidest cabinFlat floorBags reachable in flight

This is the one that feels like a small room rather than a tube. You can stand up and walk the cabin normally, the floor is flat the whole way back, and it's over a foot and a half wider than the Citations. The baggage hold is inside the pressurized cabin, so you can get to a bag mid-flight, and it's the most comfortable fit for your checked bags and Pelican cases.

My take

If standing up and space matter most, it's the Challenger 300. It's the only true stand-up cabin on the list and the easiest fit for your bags and cases.

If you'd like the best value, the Citation X is the lowest price and the fastest, and the Hawker 800XP gives you the roomiest midsize cabin for about the same money.

If bags are the main concern on a budget, look at the Sovereign+.
