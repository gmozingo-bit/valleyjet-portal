My take

If you want the best value with the most room, it's the Challenger 350 paired with the Falcon 20. It's the lowest full-trip price, with a wide cabin on the long Christmas leg.

If you'd like one aircraft and one crew the whole way, the Citation CJ3 is the simplest, and the Challenger 300 is the roomiest.

If getting there fast matters most, the Citation X is the quickest across the country.
