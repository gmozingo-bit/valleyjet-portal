Great question, and you're not alone. Most people don't know these jets by name until they've flown a few. Here's what actually separates them, so you can pick the one that feels right.

My take

If you'd like one aircraft for the whole trip at the best price, it's the Falcon 50EX.

If you want room to get up and walk around, the Challenger 300 and the Gulfstreams all give you that.

If you're happy to mix and match, the G-IVSP out and the Challenger 300 home is the lowest combination.
