My take

If value matters most, it's the TBM or the CJ2. They're the lowest-priced turboprop and jet on your list.

If you want it easy with the dogs, look at the PC-12, with its big cabin and cargo door.

If you want room to get up and walk around, the Hawker 1000A is the upgrade worth stepping up for.
